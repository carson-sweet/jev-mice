Replay — Replay

F-08 steps 1 and 2 — FR-062, FR-103, SM-03
  • Replay reads the stored record and makes no call to the decision model. That is the reproducibility claim.
  • The scrubber spans the whole run with chunk boundaries marked faintly, so a long jump is predictable.
  • Charts are complete from tick zero because they read the summary series, not the chunks.

Replay — Inspector in replay

F-08 step 3 — FR-065
  • Identical panel to the live view, sourced from the chunk currently loaded.
  • It always shows the decision current at the scrubber's tick, never a stale one from another tick.
  • This is how someone answers 'why did that mouse walk into a trap' after the fact.

Replay — Loading a chunk

F-08 step 2 — FR-102
  • Scrubbing outside the loaded range fetches one chunk, not the whole record.
  • The state is brief and local to the range; everything already loaded stays visible.
  • Chunks are immutable, so a range visited once never has to be fetched again.
