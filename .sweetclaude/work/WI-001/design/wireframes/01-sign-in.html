01 · Sign in and public arrival

Wireframe, not a mockup. How a person enters the product, with authentication required and with it switched off. Covers F-01 and F-02. Neutral styling; no visual design has been chosen yet.

Sign in and public arrival — Sign in

F-01 step 1 — FR-075, FR-076, FR-080
  • One action only. No email or password path exists.
  • The privacy notice appears before sign-in, not after, because FR-080 requires it up front.
  • Scopes requested are openid, email and profile and no others (FR-075).

Watch a colony of mice, cats and traps where every animal's decisions are made by a calibrated decision model, and see exactly what it was asked and what it answered.

What is stored. Your Google account id, email address, name and profile picture. Nothing else about you, and no tracking of any kind.

Sign in and public arrival — Sign-in failed

F-01 step 3 error — FR-082
  • No account is created on a failed or cancelled attempt.
  • The message says the attempt can be retried and does not blame the person.
  • The same screen is used when the identity provider errors and when the person cancels.
